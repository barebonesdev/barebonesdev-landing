Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the WA Driver License Appointment Finder app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built this app to be as simple and privacy-respecting as possible.

We Do Not Collect Any Data

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising identifiers built into the App.

How the App Works

The App monitors publicly available appointment availability on the official Washington State Department of Licensing (WA DOL) website and notifies you when an earlier slot appears. When you choose to book an appointment, the App opens a web view that displays the official WA DOL website directly. Any information you enter at that point (such as your name, contact details, or appointment selections) is sent directly to WA DOL and is governed by WA DOL's own privacy practices, not by this App.

Notifications

Alerts generated by the App are based solely on publicly available appointment availability and are delivered locally on your device. No personal data is attached to or required for these notifications.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external destination the App links to is the official WA DOL website.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@barebonesdev.net.
